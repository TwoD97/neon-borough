# Art

All raster artwork in this layout (the crew robots in `mods/crew/chars/`, the town in `mods/crew/town/`, backgrounds, component shells, keycaps, rails and cards) is original work created by the author with ChatGPT image generation. The author directed, selected, cut and assembled it. The prompts used are kept next to the images (`mods/crew/art/*prompts*.json`, `mods/my-spaces/art/*prompt*.json`, and the town generation briefs).

Vector fittings and icons (`*.svg`) were drawn for this layout, except where a folder carries its own license (Pixelarticons in `mods/my-spaces/icons/pixelart/`).

The artwork is covered by the repository's license (see LICENSE).
